# App Review Notes

Contact: Piotr Kaminski · contact@kamilunavo.com · +49 151 40571562

The app works without an account. Complete onboarding and tap a sample card. The camera button tests optional photo proof. For sharing, sign in to iCloud, enable iCloud Sync in Settings, then choose “Invite household members.” The system CloudKit sharing sheet opens. The interactive widget can be added from the Home Screen.

- `com.kamilunavo.schonerledigt.pro.yearly` — auto-renewable annual subscription
- `com.kamilunavo.schonerledigt.pro.lifetime` — non-consumable lifetime purchase

Free: six cards, 30 days of history, widgets. Pro: unlimited cards, shared iCloud households, photo proof and complete history.
