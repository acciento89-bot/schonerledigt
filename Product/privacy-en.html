Privacy Policy

Effective: August 30, 2026

Already Done? processes cards, confirmations, the name you enter, and optional photos on your iPhone. Without iCloud sync, this content does not leave your device.

When iCloud sync or sharing is enabled, Apple processes this content in your private CloudKit area under Apple's terms. Purchases use the App Store. The app has no ads, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Content remains stored until you delete individual cards, remove the app or its data, or manage your private iCloud data and shares through your Apple ID. You can disable iCloud sync and notifications in the app at any time. Because no account is created with us, we do not hold account credentials.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: contact@kamilunavo.com.
