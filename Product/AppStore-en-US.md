## Description

Was the front door locked? Did someone feed the pet? Already Done? gives you and your household one clear answer. Create recurring cards, confirm them with a tap, and see who did what and when. Each card automatically becomes open again on your schedule.

Features: custom cards, history, automatic resets, optional photo proof, private iCloud households, reminders, interactive widgets, local use without an account, German and English.

Without iCloud, content stays on the iPhone. No ads or tracking. The app records input and is not a safety, medical, or emergency system.
