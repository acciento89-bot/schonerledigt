# Product: Schon erledigt?

## Promise

A calm, immediate answer to recurring everyday questions: what is open, what was confirmed, by whom and when?

## Audience

Singles, couples, families, shared households and caregivers. This is intentionally not a project manager.

## Business model 1.0

- Free: up to six cards, 30-day history and interactive widgets.
- Pro: unlimited cards, private iCloud household, full history and photo proof.
- Planned App Store price: €14.99/year or €29.99 lifetime.

Apple Watch, spoken Siri commands and Lock Screen widgets are not promised in 1.0.
